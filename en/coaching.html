About Ngô Tấn Phát

Meet Ngô Tấn Phát.

Vietnamese voice actor, dubbing director and voice coach. Began my professional career at Dat Phi Media in 2013.

My professional journey

I began studying dubbing in mid-2006 and later trained under voice director Xuân Tâm. In 2013, I joined Dat Phi Media and began my professional voice-acting career.

Since June 2025, I have worked as a dubbing director at Dat Phi Media; since September 2025, as a voice instructor at Dat Phi Academy.

Drawing on my experience as an actor and director, I help learners build real connection when speaking and reading, or explore character psychology in dubbing. The two programmes have distinct focuses shaped around each learner’s goals.

Before we talk

A few useful details.

How do voice training and dubbing coaching differ?

Voice training focuses on speaking and reading to connect with listeners in professional and creative settings. Dubbing coaching focuses on psychological acting and character embodiment.

What are the schedules and fees?

Contact me to discuss your goals, 1:1 or group learning and a suitable schedule. In-person classroom options are handled through Dat Phi Academy.

What should I share?

The programme you are interested in, how you use your voice, your experience, goals and availability. You may include a recording link if you have one.
