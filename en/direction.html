About Ngô Tấn Phát

Meet Ngô Tấn Phát.

Vietnamese voice actor, dubbing director and voice coach. Began my professional career at Dat Phi Media in 2013.

My professional journey

My professional dubbing career began at Dat Phi Media in 2013, following studies starting in 2006 and training under voice director Xuân Tâm.

Since June 2025, I have worked as a dubbing director at Dat Phi Media; since September 2025, as a voice instructor at Dat Phi Academy.

My experience performing characters informs how I work with actors on circumstances, psychology and the intention of each line. I personally lead direction and casting, and assemble a production team around each project.
