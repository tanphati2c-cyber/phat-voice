RECORDING ESSENTIALS

Before we begin.

01

48-hour TVC delivery.

TVC recordings are delivered within 48 hours after the content and scope of work are confirmed. Delivery timelines for longer projects or projects requiring studio coordination are agreed separately.

02

Two free revisions.

Up to two revision rounds are included at no extra charge. From the third round, fees are based on the proportion of revised words relative to the total word count.

03

Changes over 50%.

After the two free revision rounds, changes exceeding 50% of the total word count are treated as a new job. The fee is discussed before work proceeds.
